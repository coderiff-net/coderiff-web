title: A landing page should make the next step feel easy
excerpt: A few simple principles for welcoming visitors, telling a clear story and helping good ideas find their people.
category: Studio notes
tags:

---

A landing page is a small invitation. Someone arrives with a question, a hunch or a little curiosity. The page has a moment to help them feel they are in the right place.

That starts with clarity. Say who the work is for, what it helps them do and why it matters. Use words a real person would say across a table. Let the layout give those words room to breathe.

Then make the next step obvious. A page does not need a dozen competing buttons or a long list of claims. It needs a natural way to continue the conversation, learn more or simply look around.

The best landing pages feel less like a pitch and more like a good first conversation: honest, focused and easy to follow.
